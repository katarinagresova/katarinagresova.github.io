My work is the loop from experiment to model to biological insight: benchmarks
and data preparation at one end, model interpretation at the other, models of
RNA regulation in between.

Training a good model is hard, and it is the part that gets all the attention.
But on flawed data, or with no way to see what it learned, it still isn't
usable — and if it isn't usable, why did we build it?

I was a software engineer before I was a scientist — four companies, Oracle and
Tieto among them — and I have learned a new field every few years since. What I
want next is a company where that whole combination is the job: owning a
research question, being the one who checks whether the answer is real, and
explaining both to the people who weren't in the room.
