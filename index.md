I work at the point where computer science runs into molecular biology:
training models that predict how small RNAs find their targets, and building
the datasets the field needs to tell a good model from a lucky one.

Before research I spent several years as a software engineer and machine
learning specialist. That still shapes how I work — reproducibly, in the open,
and with a healthy suspicion of numbers that look too good.
